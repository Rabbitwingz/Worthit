FAQ

Good questions.

Is this financial advice?+

Nope. Worth It is a personal awareness tool. The worth-it score is a decision aid built from your own answers, not a recommendation, and it never presents returns as guaranteed.

Where is my data stored?+

Only on your phone, in the app's private storage. There's no account and no server. You can export it as JSON or delete everything from the You tab.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?+

Not yet! Every build is made automatically on GitHub from the open source code and published as a signed APK, so you can see exactly what you're installing.

Does it work with my currency?+

It's INR-first (with lakh and crore formatting), and also supports USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, AUD, CAD, SGD and AED. It doesn't convert between currencies.

I'm a freelancer with an irregular income. Does it still work?+

Yes. Use a rough monthly average and turn on “irregular schedule”. Prices are then shown in calendar-day income, and the app always tells you which basis it's using.
